About the Center

The Center for Kinetic Calculations is a page dedicated to development of methodological apparatus focused on solid-state kinetic analysis. The provided softwares are free to use, distribute, and modify – any suggestions on their improvement or bug reports are most welcome. The Publications section contains relevant scientific papers and other texts.

About the Author

Ing. Roman Svoboda, PhD. is currently employed at University of Pardubice (Czech Republic) and at University of Hradec Králové (Czech Republic). He is mainly interested in thermo-analytical, microscopic and spectroscopic techniques, theory of glassy state, solid-state kinetics, and dissolution kinetics. Material-wise, he is focused on polymers, pharmaceuticals and amorphous chalcogenides.
